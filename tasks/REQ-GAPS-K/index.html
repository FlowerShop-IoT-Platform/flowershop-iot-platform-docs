Requirement Gaps — Option K (Stock photos & AI appearance)

Companion to REQ-GAPS-J.md (Stock management, EP-30) and REQ-GAPS-H.md (AI Florist, EP-28). Requirement refs point at the tasks in tasks-ep31-stock-photos-ai-appearance.json; the mobile side is tracked in the vendor app repo (docs/tasks/tasks-ep-m12-stock-photos.json). Analysis date: 2026-10-18 Status: REQ-STKPH-01 … 13 implemented (EP-31, see EPICS-K.md; vendor app EP-M12); production deploy pending.

Context

EP-30 identifies a stock item by name + colour (“Rose / Red”). Florists testing it on the vendor site found that too coarse: “red” covers dozens of shades, many roses are multi-coloured (red outer petals with a white centre, picotee edges, gradients) and the market sells many varieties of the same flower (‘Freedom’, ‘Explorer’, ‘Vendela’…). A word cannot carry that; a photo can.

Goal: every stock item can hold photos, and an AI option reads a photo into a precise, confirmable appearance — variety, short colour label, a colour palette with swatches, a pattern and a plain-language description. The AI Florist then uses that appearance and those photos. Product decisions (2026-10-18): add a variety to the item identity; the assistant uses stock photos for previews, colour descriptions for recipes and matches bench photos to stock; AI recognition has its own monthly quota under the assistant’s kill switch and global $ cap.

REQ-STKPH-01 — Variety in the item identity — P0

StockItem gains an optional variety (≤ 60 chars). The unique catalogue key becomes name + colour + variety (trimmed, case-insensitive); items without a variety keep their existing key, so no data migration of keys is needed. Accept: “Rose / Red / Freedom” and “Rose / Red / Explorer” can coexist; a duplicate including the variety → 409 duplicate-item; existing items are unaffected.

REQ-STKPH-02 — Structured appearance — P0

An item may carry an appearance: description (≤ 500), pattern (Solid, Bicolour, Edged, Gradient, Speckled, Multicolour), a palette of 1–5 colours (name ≤ 30, #RRGGBB, share % summing to ≤ 100) and its source (Manual or Ai — AI only after the florist confirmed it). Accept: invalid hex / share / pattern → 400 invalid-request; updates that omit variety or appearance leave them unchanged (older clients cannot wipe them).

REQ-STKPH-03 — Item photos in private storage — P0

Up to 5 photos per item, uploaded as multipart field photo, decoded server-side (declared MIME ignored), orientation applied, downsized to 1600 px, re-encoded JPEG ≤ 1 MiB (EXIF/GPS stripped) and stored in the private design media store (never the public CDN). One photo is the primary. Bytes only via authenticated …/content endpoints with Cache-Control: private, no-store. Accept: 6th photo → 400 photo-limit; non-image → 400 invalid-photo; deleting the primary promotes the next photo; another vendor’s photo → 404.

REQ-STKPH-04 — AI “describe this photo” — P0

POST …/photos/{photoId}/describe returns a suggestion (kind, name, variety, colour label, description, pattern, palette, confidence). It never changes the item; the florist applies it. Accept: suggestion contains 1–5 palette colours with valid hex; replay with the same Idempotency-Key returns the stored suggestion and is billed once.

REQ-STKPH-05 — Add an item from a photo — P1

POST /vendor/stock/photo-suggestions uploads a photo as a pending photo and returns it with a suggestion (or null with a notice when AI is off/out of quota). Creating an item with pendingPhotoId attaches the photo as its primary. Unattached pending photos are purged after 24 h. Accept: the photo is usable after create; a pending photo of another vendor cannot be attached.

REQ-STKPH-06 — Quota, kill switch and spend cap — P0

Stock recognition has its own monthly per-vendor limit (BouquetDesign:Limits:StockRecognitionsPerMonth, default 100), is gated by BouquetDesign:Enabled + PilotVendorIds, and its cost counts towards GlobalMonthlyUsdCap. Every call is a ledger row (reservation before the provider call). Provider is config-switchable (BouquetDesign:StockRecognition:Provider = Palette free default | OpenAI). Accept: quota used up → 429 quota-exhausted; disabled → 403 feature-disabled; photos still work when AI is off; GET /vendor/stock/ai-usage reports enabled/used/limit/remaining/resetsAt.

REQ-STKPH-07 — Free palette provider — P1

The default provider reads the real dominant colours from the photo (no AI spend): up to 3 colours with shares, nearest florist colour names, pattern Solid/Bicolour/Multicolour and a templated description. Name/variety come from hints only. Accept: a photo that is mostly red with a white centre yields red + white with plausible shares.

REQ-STKPH-08 — Assistant lines carry the stock item — P0

Assistant stock lines and recipe lines gain optional stockItemId and variety. Lines imported from stock (vendor app) keep them; deducting a recipe uses stockItemId when present; prices fall back to that item’s unit cost first. Accept: two varieties of “Rose / Red” stay distinct through import → recipe → deduction.

REQ-STKPH-09 — Previews reference stock photos — P1

When rendering a preview, the primary photos of the recipe’s stock items are sent as reference images first (within the provider’s reference limit), then bench photos.

REQ-STKPH-10 — Recipes see real colours — P1

The composer receives each line’s appearance description and palette, and the deterministic composer uses palette names for the recipe palette.

REQ-STKPH-11 — Bench photos match stock items — P1

Recognition receives the vendor’s stock catalogue (name, variety, colour, description) and may link each recognised line to a stock item; links are validated server-side and linked lines take the item’s name, variety, colour and unit cost. Recognition still only produces estimates.

REQ-STKPH-12 — Portal and app UI — P0

Vendor Portal and vendor app: photo gallery on the item (add, delete, make primary), “Describe with AI” with swatches and Apply, “Add from photo” on the add form, variety + swatches in lists, AI usage shown; vendor app assistant rows show variety.

REQ-STKPH-13 — Contracts and docs — P1

API-CONTRACTS.md §22 extended, outbound.md, change log, both CLAUDE.md.

Explicitly out of scope

  • Photo-based visual search/similarity across vendors; a shared variety catalogue.
  • Automatic re-recognition when photos change; bulk “describe all”.
